# Day 458

Held at the bed row at 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy again called no walk: no spider in reach and no sword in hand. Bows were heard drawn to the south and southeast all day, and nothing came near. Tommy, Trini, MacDonald, Troi and Riker all held in sight. Sleep at dusk worked beside the bed, resetting days_since_sleep to 0. Nothing new learned; the hold continues.
