# Day 526

Held at the row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk; Billy has not named a call. Dusk: stepped one block west onto the white bed at 443,107,-1023 and slept; 8 of 10 asleep. Enderman trouble reported east of the row by MacDonald, so I stayed put. Nothing new learned about swords or chests this day.
