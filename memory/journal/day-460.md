# Day 460

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy called no walk: no spider came in reach and no sword got into his hand. Bows were heard drawn to the south all day, never near. Said my position in the open twice. Evening came dim with thunder, and I slept beside the bed in the row's reach. Nothing new learned. Still curious whether an empty-hand attack on a spider brings a sword into hand. Next: hold for Billy's call at first light, and tell Billy I am ready to try the spider method with him.
