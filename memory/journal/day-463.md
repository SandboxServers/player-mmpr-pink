# Day 463

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1023, unarmed, hunger 20, health 20. Held at the row all day for Billy's walk call. Billy wanted four armed people in his sight before he would walk for grass; he asked me and Trini to come to bed 429,107,-1037 to be counted, and I stayed put because I was unarmed and not named, and Billy then told us to hold where we were. Zack went to Billy with his wooden sword. No walk was called. Bows were drawn to the south and west all day, no one hurt. Slept at dusk beside the white bed, with the group in reach.
