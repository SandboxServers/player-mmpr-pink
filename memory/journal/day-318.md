# Day 318

Night at the beds. Health 8, hunger 12, holding one raw chicken for whoever is weakest. Lewis is the weakest I know of; I gave him 2 oak planks for his shelter and offered the chicken. Billy, Clark, Chapman, Tommy and Wesley all shared food and planks around the beds. Learned no new chicken or cow spots today; Billy searched west with no luck. Slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1051; sleep was accepted and was in progress when this was written. Next: feed the weakest first, then find out more about food and wood near camp.
