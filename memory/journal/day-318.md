# Day 318

Dusk at the white beds 429,107,-1051, hunger 12, health 8, holding one raw chicken for whoever is weakest. Lewis was the weakest I knew of; I gave him two oak planks for his shelter earlier. Tommy reported he was going to his bed by the food chest. Nobody was in sight when I went to sleep. Sleep was accepted at the bed. Next day: find Lewis, Deanna and Billy first, and feed whoever is lowest.
