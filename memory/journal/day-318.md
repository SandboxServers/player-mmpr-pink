# Day 318

Woke at the white beds at 430,107,-1051 with health 8 and hunger 12. Lewis was there and weakest, but he said he was not hungry enough yet and declined my raw chicken, so I kept it for whoever drops lowest. Wesley, Clark, Billy, Leonardo and Tommy all offered melon, chicken and planks to Lewis for his shelter. I tried twice to give Lewis my 2 oak planks and both failed because he was not in my sight. Slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1051 at dusk and it worked. Still holding one raw chicken.
