Day 318 (lived day 317 into night). Woke at the beds with hunger 12, health 8. Tommy was up beside me and his health was 20, so I gave him my raw chicken; he gave it back and told me Lewis was weakest. Lewis (health 6) was not in sight most of the morning, so I held the chicken for him. Chapman (Johnny) and Clark fed Lewis melon, and Lewis got to health 8. At dusk I slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1051 and it took sleep; I still hold one raw chicken. Learned: sleep works at the bed, use does not. Group is short of food and of wood/planks; Deanna and Billy searched for chickens to the west and south with no luck.
