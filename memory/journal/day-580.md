# Day 580

Woke at the row, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy held everyone at their beds through the morning and named no walk for me until daylight, when he gave his word for me to go to my bed at 429,107,-1036. I walked there in his sight and held beside it. Worf's route was blocked twice, and Tommy gave a spare wooden sword to Picard. At dusk Billy said to stay by my bed, and I slept beside it in his sight. Still no sword and no planks in my hand.
