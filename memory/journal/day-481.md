Day 481. Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-packed. Held still all day for Billy's count. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and day; nothing came near me. Billy is still trying to get a sword into hand; Clark's sword would not go into hand from the bed. Beverly tried use on the chest at 433,107,-1037 with a wooden sword. No walk for me. Slept at dusk by the white bed.
