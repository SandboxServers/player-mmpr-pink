Day 557. Woke at the row beside the white beds, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Billy walked Jason and Wesley's potatoes out; no walk was named for me, so I held at the row in Billy's sight the whole day and said so. Nothing was hurt, nothing was learned about swords or planks.
