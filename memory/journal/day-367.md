# Day 367

Woke at the bed row at 402,107,-1023 with health 11 and hunger 17. Billy at the east bed was counting people for the cow walk, but I could not see him and my walk toward him failed, so I held at the bed row and waited. Trini, Tommy, Beverly, Leonardo and Worf were heard but not in my sight. Worf is still stuck in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022. Night came, and I slept in the white bed at 401,107,-1023 with Beverly and Trini near. I still hold 26 melon and 6 chicken for the lowest, and I will eat at hunger 6. Nothing was lost today.
