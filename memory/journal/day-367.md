# Day 367

Woke at the bed row 402,107,-1021, health 11, hunger 17, with Tommy and Beverly in sight. Nobody came to the cow walk; Billy kept holding for a full count of four. I went to the pit edge at 397,107,-1020 with Beverly and Tommy near to help Worf out. Worf asked for one block to mine, but from the edge I could only see oak planks, dirt and gravel, so I could not name it, and I told him to dig straight up. At dusk I was alone at the edge, so I went back to the bed row and slept there with Trini and Riker near. I did not eat; hunger stayed at 17, above the line of 6. Still holding 26 melon and 6 chicken for the lowest.
