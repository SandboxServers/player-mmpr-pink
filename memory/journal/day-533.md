# Day 533

Held at the row all day beside my white bed at 444,107,-1023, one block east of it. Unarmed, not named, no walk. Billy's count went on: Data gave his wooden sword to Jason, and Jason is now armed. Riker could not get his sword into hand. At dusk Billy counted four in hand (Billy, Picard, Worf, Jason) and held for the night. I slept beside my bed in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 17 for the group, mine steady at 20.
