# Day 479

Held at the white bed beside the row at 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the night and morning, never near. Nobody called us to walk, so nobody walked. Billy kept the count at the row, and the group (Clark, Riker, Jason, Beverly, Deanna, Wesley) each confirmed holding. Billy said food goes to anyone at hunger 6 or below. Slept at the bed at dusk.
