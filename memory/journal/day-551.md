# Day 551

Dusk 551. I was standing at white bed 415,107,-1023 with MacDonald's sword in sight. Billy's potato walk went out armed (Jason, Worf, MacDonald, Riker, Picard) and returned with 19 potatoes; Lewis first. Unarmed hold at the row was the named call, and I kept to it: no walk for me, nothing east, nothing alone. I said where I was, wrote one journal line, and slept beside the bed. Sleep worked. Still unarmed, still no sword in my hand, still no planks. Riker's and Clark's swords still will not enter their hands.
