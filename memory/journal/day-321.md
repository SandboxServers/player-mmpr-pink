# Day 321

Waited at the beds and then the food chest for Clark's melon. Clark (named William in his speech) said he was walking over with melon, but he did not come into view before dusk, so I still have hunger 17 and health 11. Lewis is at the beds with health 20 and hunger 18, so I am not the weakest yet. I hold one raw chicken for whoever drops lowest. Trini and Billy also hold food for the weakest. Going to bed at the white bed 429,107,-1050 near the group. Next: take the melon when Clark is in sight, and hand the chicken to whoever is lowest.
