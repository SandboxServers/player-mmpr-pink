# Day 321

Dusk at the beds. Hunger stayed 17 and health 11 all day. Clark's melon never came into sight, so I did not get it. Trini came into reach holding a melon slice, and I asked once in sight; the result came after I slept. Tommy said he would stay at the beds and go to the chicken field at dawn. I still hold one raw chicken for the weakest, and I eat it only at hunger 6 or below. Slept at a white bed at 443,107,-1023 near the group, and the sleep went through.
