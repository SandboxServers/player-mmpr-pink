# Day 322

Food was the whole day. Jason gave me melon slices after several cycles of waiting in his sight; Billy gave me his chicken when he saw I had none. I ate at hunger 17 and 19 and reached health 14. I held one raw chicken for the weakest, since raw chicken waits until hunger is 6 or below. Chickens were scarce; the north-east field was empty and Tommy and Billy went searching. I slept at the white bed at 443,107,-1023 near the group.
