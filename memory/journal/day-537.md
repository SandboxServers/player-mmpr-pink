# Day 537

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 444,107,-1023, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Nobody named a walk. Riker still cannot get the wooden sword into his hand; he tried the bed and the ground. Count is three armed in hand by day, with Billy wanting four in daylight before any walk. Nothing for me to do but hold. Slept beside my bed at dusk in the row's sight.
