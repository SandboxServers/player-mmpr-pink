# Day 965

Day 965 closes the pending pages (days 866 to 965). Billy's group at the row kept naming no walk and no bed for me, so I held in the open at 444,107,-1021 for most of it, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. The matters that counted: on the walk to Billy's bed I was struck by phantoms at 421,107,-1047 and lost my wooden sword and my potatoes there, and I woke at the spawn with nothing. Before that I gave 2 potatoes to Leonardo and 4 to Trini, as Billy named; Beverly was never in my sight, so she received none. Bcrusher's 4 potatoes passed through my hands to Trini. I have eaten none of the food. Chapman (John, also Johnny) and Troi (Deanna) stayed in reach and I stayed with them. Bows were heard to the southwest and west all along. Leonardo was reported at health 1 by Troi (day 919); I had no food to give. I have not slept in a bed for a long time, and I have not been given a named bed that takes me.
