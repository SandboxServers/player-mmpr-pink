# Day 402

Held at the bed row all day beside the white bed at 444,107,-1037 with Jason, Trini and Troi in sight. No cow walk: Billy did not call one. Health held at 6.3, hunger 17, so I kept my 27 melon and 9 chicken for the lowest. Counted myself at first light on day 401 and again at dusk on day 402. Went to sleep at the bed 443,107,-1037 with Troi and Jason in reach. Worf's pit cell is still unnamed; I dug nothing.
