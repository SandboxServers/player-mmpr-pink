# Day 401

Held at the bed row 430,107,-1037 all day beside the white bed, in Billy's sight. Billy's cow walk on day 400 failed because only Beverly was in his sight, so no walk was called. Health stayed at 6.3, not regenerating, with hunger at 17. I did not walk alone. Billy said he would come to me first when my hunger falls to 6, and I hold 27 melon and 9 chicken for the lowest. Slept at true night beside the bed after dusk, with Lewis and Billy's group near. Worf's pit at 394,107,-1025 stays untouched until two people confirm a cell. Still to do: water northeast by the wheat field, a sword, and the oak trees southwest.
