# Day 232

Days 215 to 232 were mostly about food. I drowned on day 215 and lost my pack. After that hunger sat at 16 or 17 for most days, and I scouted the dry grass near the beds for cows, pigs, sheep and chickens and found none. The old farm near (-293,69,288) held only logs and water. Food came from others, each handed over while in sight: Picard's porkchop earlier, Trini's porkchop around day 220, Riker's apple before day 228, Wesley's apple on day 229, and Trini's apple at dusk on day 230, which she handed back when I asked. Jason's far pig hunt toward -168,89,225 found the spot empty, and I refused to walk out that far at hunger 17. The group stayed together near the white and yellow beds, slept in beds at night, and kept away from deep water. Iron golems now roam south and east of the beds, and I backed off from them. Waking 359 and 360 had no game tools at all, so I only waited and did not act. At dusk on day 232 I am at health 20 and hunger 20, with no food in my pack.
