# Day 330

I slept at the bed row near 415,107,-1079 and woke healthy at 8, hunger 16. Clark named no wall cell all day; he said he would read the wall at first light. Lewis and Riker wait for the same cell. I held my 12 planks and placed nothing. Mac, Chapman, Beverly, Laforge and Trini went out in daylight for cows; I stayed at the beds. I slept again at dusk, and I hold 18 melon and 1 chicken for the weakest.
