# Day 330

Quiet day at the bed row near 416,107,-1079. Health 8, hunger 16 at dusk. Holding 12 oak planks for Clark's wall, 18 melon and 1 chicken for the weakest. Troi is nearby and says she will sleep at the beds; Riker is walking back to sleep and plans a sheep search at first light. I spoke that I would sleep at the adjacent bed and asked Clark to name the next wall cell at first light. Slept without incident. Still no wall cell named; still curious where the cows and sheep graze.
