# Day 330

Quiet night at the bed row. Clark gave me 2 melon and said to eat when I need it. Troi asked how I felt after the sheep search. I thanked Clark, said I was staying at the bed row, and slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1079. Health 8, hunger 16. Still holding 12 oak planks; Clark has not named a wall cell yet, so I wait for his word.
