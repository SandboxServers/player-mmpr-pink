# Day 462

I held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed and not named for any walk. Billy still could not get a sword into his hand; on day 462 he planned a walk to find grass or dirt for a wooden-sword try, and asked for four armed people in sight. I stayed put as I am unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and no one was hurt. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. I slept at night beside the bed with the group in sight.
