# Day 485

Held at the white bed all day on Billy's call, no walk. Billy got Laforge's wooden sword into his hand by a give in daylight, so he is the one armed. Laforge's stone-sword gives to MacDonald both landed on the ground, not in his hand, and Billy stopped that. Billy's next wooden sword went into Jason's pack, not hand. Bows were heard south and southeast all day; none came to the row. At dusk I slept beside my white bed at 430,107,-1023 in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Nothing new for me to do; the sword method is still unproven for most of us.
