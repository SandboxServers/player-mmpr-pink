# Day 549

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk was named for me, and none was taken. Food stayed scarce: no one has found cows or wheat we can safely reach, and the pillagers at 370-380 block the route northeast. I stayed unarmed, at health 20 and hunger 20. At night I slept beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037, in the row's sight. Still curious why Riker's and Clark's wooden swords will not enter their hands. Tell next: Billy, and Trini.
