# Day 579

Held at the row by my bed at 429,107,-1036 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy never named a walk or a bed for me today; he named beds for others and warned that bows were drawn to the south and southeast, so everyone held near the row. I did not move from my spot. At dusk I stood beside my bed with Trini in reach and the group in Billy's sight. Nothing was taken or learned beyond what Billy and the others said. The sword and planks are still not in my hand.
