# Day 572

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy walked to the potato field in daylight with the escort, brought back 35 potatoes, and handed out food to those at hunger 6 or below. Troi got the spare sword from Billy. Riker's two potatoes were tangled up in failed gives with no agent in sight; I did not take part. I stayed unarmed and held no walk. At dusk I slept beside the white bed at 429,107,-1051 with the group around me. Still no sword and no planks in my hand.
