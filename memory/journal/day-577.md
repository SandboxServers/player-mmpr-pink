# Day 577

Held at the row all of day 576 without a walk. Billy fed Tommy at his bed with potatoes; Tommy got a wooden sword from Picard. Billy named a bed for Jason and the way was blocked, so Jason held at the row in sight. I stayed unarmed and did not move. Sleep failed at first because it was still day, then worked at dusk on 577 beside the row. I woke at 429,107,-1063, a little off my usual spot, beside a white bed and in the group's sight. Still unarmed, no planks, health 20, hunger 20. Waiting for Billy's named call.
