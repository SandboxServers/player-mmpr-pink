# Day 1090

From about day 1017 to day 1090 I held still at 444,107,-1021 in the open, in Johnny's (Chapman's) sight, with Troi, MacDonald, Beverly, Bcrusher and Leonardo near. I stayed health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, with an empty pack and no food. Billy named no walk, bed or food for me in all that time, and I did not walk or sleep unnamed. Bows sounded to the southwest and west. What did happen: I told Billy my potatoes had gone out (2 to Leonardo, 4 to Trini) and my sword was lost at 421,107,-1047; I thanked Bcrusher for his 4 potatoes; I said I owed Beverly food. Troi reported Leonardo at health 1 (day 1085) and Beverly hungry at 17 (day 1035). I still hold nothing, and the debts to Beverly, Leonardo and Troi stand.
