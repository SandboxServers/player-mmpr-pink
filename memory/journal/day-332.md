# Day 332

Walked to the wall at 423,107,-1079 and gave Clark the gap cell 423,107,-1078. Lewis placed one plank there on Clark's word. By dusk no further open gap was named, and Clark asked me to stand in the gap and give exact coordinates at first light. Health 8, hunger 16. Bcrusher, Troi and Laforge went to the cow spot at 370,93,-1124 in daylight; I did not join. At dusk I said I would not walk to the wall in the dark, and I slept at the bed row beside the white bed 415,107,-1078.
