# Day 332

Woke at the bed row with health 8 and hunger 16. Walked to the wall and gave Clark the gap cell 423,107,-1078. Lewis placed one plank there on Clark's word; I placed nothing, since my view of the wall is too rough to name a cell. Clark found no open gap by dusk. An enderman was seen south-east, so I went back to the bed row and slept beside bed 415,107,-1078. What mattered: I hold the planks only for the cell Clark names, and I say plainly when I cannot see well enough.
