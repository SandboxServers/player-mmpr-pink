# Day 482

Held at the bed at 430,107,-1023 all day for Billy's count. Bows were drawn to the south, none came near. Laforge sleeping at his bed nearby in Billy's sight. No walk, no east, no errand. At dusk I noted my position and went to sleep beside the white bed, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Nothing new learned; still no sword, no planks, no sealed shelter.
