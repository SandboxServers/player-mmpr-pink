# Day 482

Held at the white bed 429,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, no walk, as Billy called. Nothing hurt me. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, none came near. Billy still has no sword in hand and no wood; Clark, Riker, Laforge and Beverly hold at the row too. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight. Health 20, hunger 20.
