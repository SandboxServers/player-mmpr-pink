# Day 578

Held at the row through the night of 577 at 430,107,-1065, unarmed, waiting for Billy's named call. Billy named my bed as 429,107,-1036 at dusk; I did not walk in the dark. In daylight of 578, on Billy's word and in his sight, I walked to it and stood beside it with Trini and Riker in reach. Nothing was hurt, health 20, hunger 20. Still no sword and no planks in my hand. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1036 at night, following goal 4.
