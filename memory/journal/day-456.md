# Day 456

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy's count for the walk to the row has not come yet; he waits for a spider in reach to try the empty-hand attack Zack described. No spider came near me. Billy, Tommy, Trini, Troi, MacDonald, Riker and Zack all held in place. At dusk a phantom was seen in the sky, and a bow was drawn to the southwest. I stayed by the bed, did not walk, and slept beside it. Nothing new is learned about the sword yet.
