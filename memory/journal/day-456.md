# Day 456

Held at the white bed by the 387-445 row all day, unarmed and without food, health 20, hunger 20. Billy called no walk: no spider in reach and no sword in hand. Tommy, Trini, Zack, Riker and MacDonald all held at the row for his call. Nothing was learned about an empty-hand attack, since no mob came close. I slept beside the bed at dusk, as planned.
