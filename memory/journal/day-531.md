# Day 531

Held at the row all day at 444,107,-1023, one block east of my white bed. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Billy counted the row (armed: Billy with a wooden sword; unarmed and holding: me, Tommy, Troi, Riker, Laforge, MacDonald). Nobody had food. Picard walked to the row and gave Billy two rotten flesh through MacDonald; Billy keeps them until his hunger is 6. I did not walk and was not named. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
