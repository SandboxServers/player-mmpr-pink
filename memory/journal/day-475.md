# Day 475

I held at the bed row all day beside white bed 429,107,-1023, unarmed, hunger 20, health 20, no walk. Billy's count stayed at two armed (Laforge, Trini), so we did not walk. Bows were drawn often to the south and southeast, none near me. At dusk a phantom came to the row and Trini fought it at health 3; Troi and Billy stayed in place. My sleep call returned without taking, so I am still awake, and I stay put by the bed in the group's sight.
