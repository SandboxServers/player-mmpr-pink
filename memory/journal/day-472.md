# Day 472

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no walk. Billy kept his list empty and gave no count of four, so nobody moved. Trini says she holds a wooden sword in hand from the death spot; Picard is at health 6 and waits for hunger 6 before any flesh. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day, none near. Slept at dusk beside the bed with Worf, Troi and Billy in sight; digest clean.
