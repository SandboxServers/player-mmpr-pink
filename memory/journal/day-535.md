# Day 535

Held at the row all day at 444,107,-1023, one block east of my bed, with no walk named for me. Billy named Data to hand the wooden sword to MacDonald; MacDonald did not reach Data before dusk and bedded at 416,107,-1023, so the sword is still with Data. Riker's sword still will not enter his hand. Armed count stays at four: Billy, Picard, Worf, Jason. I have no food, no sword and no planks to give. I slept beside my bed at night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20.
