# Day 464

Held at the bed row all day beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Billy wants four armed in sight before any walk; only Trini and Zack are armed, so no walk was called. I stayed put unarmed, as my goal says. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and nobody was hurt. Billy has no spare sword now. I slept at night beside the bed, health 20, hunger 20.
