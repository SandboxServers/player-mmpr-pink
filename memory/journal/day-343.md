# Day 343

Slept at the bed row at 430,107,-1023. Health 8, hunger 16 at sleep. The day was quiet: Trini was fed by Troi and Leonardo and holds 6 melon, so she is safe for now. Billy, Leonardo, Wesley and Jason walked for cows toward 339,-979 and came back empty-handed, with bows heard to the south all day. I stayed at the beds with my 29 melon and did not walk out alone. I declined Troi's request to eat at hunger 16, keeping to my rule of eating at 6 or below. Jason returned with 4 beef and 3 chicken and asked who was weakest. Tommy named Trini and me. I still hold the 12 oak planks for Clark's wall and did not place them, since Clark has named no cell. I asked Billy how to build a sealed shelter, and no one has answered yet.
