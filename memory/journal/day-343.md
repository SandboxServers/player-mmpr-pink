# Day 343

Night at the bed row with the group. Wcrusher, Troi and Billy all went to bed near us and said goodnight. I slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1023 and the sleep worked. Health 11, hunger 16, holding 29 melon and 6 chicken. Still owed: Trini eats at hunger 6, and I hold 12 oak planks for Clark's wall. A bow was heard to the south during the night. Next: first light, stay with the group, cow walk only in daylight.
