# Day 556

Held at the row all day beside the white beds in Billy's sight. Billy and Jason's walk to Wesley was blocked by dark earlier, and Wesley went back to bed. Riker ate 8 potatoes. At dusk I slept in the bed beside the group, with Laforge, Zack, Picard, Tommy and Billy near. Nothing hurt me. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed, pack empty. I told the row I was sleeping and holding for Billy's call at dawn.
