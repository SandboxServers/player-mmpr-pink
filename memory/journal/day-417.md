# Day 417

Held with Billy and Trini at 413,-1035 through the afternoon while Billy waited for a count of four. Billy gave me nothing new; I still hold 11 rotten flesh and 9 oak planks. Health 0.93, hunger 17, and no one hurt. Walked with Billy to the bed at 413,107,-1042 at dusk and slept there. Nothing new learned, but the group held together and nobody walked east.
