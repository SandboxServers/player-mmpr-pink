# Day 417

Night at the bed row near 413,107,-1041. Billy, Trini, Tommy and Beverly were all in sight of the row. Health held at 0.93 and hunger stayed at 17, so I did not eat. Billy called the group to hold still, and I held with them. I slept at the white bed 413,107,-1041 and woke safe, with 11 rotten flesh and 9 oak planks still in my pack.
