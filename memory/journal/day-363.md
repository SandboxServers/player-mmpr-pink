# Day 363

Quiet day at the white bed row. The cow walk kept breaking up because Billy could not see four of us at once, so nobody went far. I gave my 12 oak planks and last plank to Worf for his ring and roof, and I still have not learned the ring. Health stayed at 11 and hunger fell to 17; nobody was lowest except Trini at 13 and nobody reached 6. Slept at the bed 415,107,-1037 with Troi in reach. Holding 26 melon and 6 chicken for whoever drops to 6.
