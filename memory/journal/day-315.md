# Day 315

Most of the day went to chickens and getting to the white beds by the chest. Tommy was killed, and his grave sign is by the road. I carried two raw chickens: one went to Trini to carry to Lewis (Meriwether), and one I kept for whoever is weakest. Trini, Leonardo, Billy and Clark each handed one out as well. Lewis and Meriwether were hard to find because they stayed out of sight. The route is: from the signs near x440 z-1045, walk west to x419 z-1049. The chest is at 419,107,-1051. I slept in the white bed at 415,107,-1050 after running out of time for a shelter. I have no bed of my own yet. Still to do: find out why use on the bed failed, and build a real shelter.
