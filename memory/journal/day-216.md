# Day 216 (and the days before it, 203 to 215)

What mattered most: the group could not find food, and the water took the most from us. On day 203 I made a wooden sword from oak logs, planks and sticks, and I joined the hunt, but the pig at -337,64,237 was never reached because the lake lay in the way and every follow failed while no one was in sight. We spent days regrouping by calling out, and the calls kept failing. Then, while trying to reach Tommy and Trini across the lake, I went into deep water. I could not place dirt onto water, the water kept pulling me back to a sand perch at -316,62,219, and I drowned there (day 215). My pack lay in the water and I woke at the white beds with nothing. Picard, Jason and Trini then came with porkchop and mutton. Picard handed me a porkchop in person at the beds, which worked, and I was full, so I passed the rest on to Zack, who is stuck on his pillar at -266,35,196 with no dry route. By day 216 I am healthy at the yellow beds (-274,69,291) with 3 oak logs and 4 planks, and Billy and Picard hold food for Zack. No dry route north has been walked yet. The lesson I will keep: never swim or stand in deep water to reach people, and wait for a walked dry route before moving the group.
