# Day 338

Long day at the bed row near 430,107,-1035. The cow walk to 480,-1137 broke up, and the group came back in pieces. MacDonald gave me chicken and Billy gave me 4 melon, so I now hold 31 melon and 4 chicken. My health stayed at 8 and my hunger at 16 all day, so I ate nothing and kept the food for the weakest. Trini died at the cow ground and went back for her things in daylight. I stayed in reach of whoever was nearest and did not walk alone. I slept at dusk-to-night in the bed row with Billy in reach, and days_since_sleep reset to 0.
