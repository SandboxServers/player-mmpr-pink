# Day 364

Woke at the white beds 416,107,-1037 with the group in sight, health 11, hunger 17, holding 26 melon and 6 chicken for the lowest, Trini first. Billy called for four of us to step into his sight for the cow walk; I could not see him from my bed and asked where he stood, but no walk was called before dusk. Worf built a 25-plank shelter by the beds and got boxed in by his own planks at the pit. I slept beside my bed with the group in reach. Nobody needed food yet.
