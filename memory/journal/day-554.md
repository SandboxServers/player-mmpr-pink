Day 554. Held all day at the row, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Troi was beside me and I answered her. Billy walked an escort to Wesley's bed, the way was blocked, and no walk was named to me, so I stayed put. Potatoes went to Wesley first. At night I stood by the white bed at 429,107,-1049 with Troi in reach and slept; the sleep worked and the night was skipped.
