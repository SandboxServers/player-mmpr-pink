# Day 550

Held at the row all day. Billy's cow walk for Lewis's food, with Jason, Clark and MacDonald as the armed three, failed when Jason lost sight of Billy; the group turned back because bows were heard south. I was never named, so I stayed put. Tommy asked for a spare sword; none was free. I am still unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Slept beside my bed at 444,107,-1037 with the group in sight.
