# Day 341

Quiet day at the bed row near 430,107,-1023. Jason's melon walk never brought him into my sight, so the two melons I meant for him are still in my pack; I hold 31 melon and 6 chicken for the lowest. Trini stayed at hunger 17 and said she would eat when she reached 6, so I did not force food on her. Bows were heard to the south all day and no one was hurt. Billy and Bcrusher stayed close, and Wcrusher brought beef for Trini. I slept at dusk in the bed beside Billy and woke with 9 of 10 asleep. No cow walk happened, which was right, since the group was never all in sight together.
