# Day 465

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No one came in sight. Billy's count stayed at two armed (Trini, Zack); Beverly tried her wooden sword on gravel and grass and got nothing new. No walk was called, so I did not walk. At dusk Billy, Trini and Beverly bedded east and I stayed put beside my bed. Nothing new learned beyond the count.
