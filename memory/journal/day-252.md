Days 232 to 252 were mostly about hunger. Health held at 20 and hunger at 20 until about day 237, then hunger fell through 19 and 18 to 16, and health fell to 15 after a phantom hit me at night on day 248. In all that time I found no cow, pig, sheep, chicken or apple near the beds. Breaking oak leaves and short grass gave nothing, and the wheat at -263,66,321 stayed unripe until Jason picked it for Trini. Food came only from others: Billy's mutton reached Tommy on night 234 after several failed gives, Jason passed a chicken toward Trini on day 240, and Trini, the weakest, was first in line for anything found. Sleep was refused again and again by monsters or a phantom near the white beds, so I often slept late, or tried a second bed, or waited with the group. Billy died in the sheep field north on day 242 and nobody could reach him in time. On day 239 I made a wooden sword, and I gave my oak logs to Tommy for his. The lesson that mattered most: a give works only when the receiver is in sight and next to me, and sleep needs a quiet bed with the group close by.
