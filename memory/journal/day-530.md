# Day 530

Held at the row all day beside my white bed at 444,107,-1023, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody named me and Billy made no count, so I did not walk. Jason, Macdonald, Deanna and Riker each called in from their beds and held still too; Riker is at hunger 17 with no food and asked for bread or melon. I said my own holding line at dawn 531. At dusk I slept beside the bed and sleep worked, 9 of 10 asleep. Nothing new to learn today; the wait is the work until Billy calls.
