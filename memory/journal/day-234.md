# Day 234

No food all day. Scouted east and north-east for animals and apples; found only oak trees and oak leaves (one sapling, no apples). Hunger stayed at 20 but I had no food to share. Billy holds mutton for Tommy, who is at health 5. Back at the white bed at -270,67,318 by afternoon. Slept at dusk; the first sleep "worked" but the night had not come, and the second sleep at night worked. Lesson: a sleep at dusk may only count once it is truly night. Next: at dawn, look for food within sight of the group; keep Tommy and Billy first in line.
