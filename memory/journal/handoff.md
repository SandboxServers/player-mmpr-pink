# Handoff, day 470 (morning, daylight)

At 430,107,-1023 beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed.

Slept beside the bed through the night of day 470 (7 of 10 asleep). Phantoms came close in the night; Trini died and respawned. I stayed put.

No errand running, no walk called. Billy counts two armed in hand (Laforge, Trini); Picard and Jason have wooden swords in pack. No cow walk until four are in hand in Billy's sight.

Next: hold for Billy's count. Never walk alone, in the dark, or east. Eat Chapman's rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below. Take a sword if one is offered in Billy's sight.
