# Handoff, day 466 (night)

At 430,107,-1023 beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed. Sleep failed three times at night ("not possible"), so I did not sleep; the bed is still the place to try again.

No errand running, no walk called. Billy holds at two armed (Zack, Beverly); Trini in sight. Bows heard to the west and south.

Next: at first light, hold for Billy's call. Never walk alone, in the dark, or east. Eat Chapman's rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below.
