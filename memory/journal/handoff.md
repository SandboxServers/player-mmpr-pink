Day 331 night. At the bed row 416,107,-1077, beside white bed 415,107,-1078. Health 8, hunger 16. No errand running.
Holding: 12 oak planks (wall; place only on a cell Clark names), 20 melon and 1 chicken (for the weakest).

Next: at first light, wait for Clark's named cell. Then consider the cow hunt in daylight with the group, in sight.
