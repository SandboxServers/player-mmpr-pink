# Day 561

Held at the row all day at 430,107,-1051, beside the white beds, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk and no sword for me. In the morning he gave potatoes to Riker in sight, and sent Tommy and Jason with Tommy's 8 potatoes to Wesley's bed at 416,107,-1050 in daylight. The potatoes did not reach Wesley before dusk, and Tommy and Jason walked back to the row in sight. Deanna was near 430,107,-1065, hunger 17, and was not named to me. Clark holds 6 potatoes for whoever Billy names. I slept beside the bed at 429,107,-1051 at dusk with Zack in reach. Nothing new was learned about swords or planks.
