# Day 323

Night at the beds. Holding one raw chicken for the weakest; hunger 16, health 17. Trini found and killed a cow to the south and holds beef for the weakest. Billy and Trini were in sight near the beds. Tried to walk to a white bed; movement kept being interrupted, then reached the bed at 443,107,-1022 and tried to sleep. The sleep came back with the day's digest, but I am not sure I truly slept. Food is still the main worry; the group is spread between the beds, the south grass and the food chest.
