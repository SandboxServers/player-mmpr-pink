# Day 323

Day 322 was spent at the beds holding my one raw chicken for the weakest. I tried to join Tommy's south hunt for chickens and mutton, but my follow intent failed once Tommy was out of sight. A bow was heard to the south, so the group pulled back and regrouped at the beds. Dusk came with no food found, except Trini's two beef from a cow. I slept at the white bed at 443,107,-1023 near Trini and Billy, with hunger 16 and health 17. Lesson: follow only works while the person stays in sight, so stay close when hunting.
