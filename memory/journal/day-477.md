# Day 477

Held at the row all day with no walk, as Billy's count asked. Still no sword in anyone's hand; Billy's count stood at zero armed at midday. Stood beside the white bed at 430,107,-1023 through the night, said goodnight to Billy, Beverly and Jason. A sleep attempt at night did not take, so I still have not rested since dusk. Nothing hurt me; health 20, hunger 20 and no food yet. Billy is the one who keeps the count, and I hold with him.
