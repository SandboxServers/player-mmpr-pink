# Day 477

Held at the row beside white bed 429,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Billy kept the count: hold at the beds, no walk, no sword method yet. Deanna, Trini, Riker, Beverly and Wesley all held in his sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the night, never near. At dusk I tried "use" on the bed and it failed ("that block is a bed"); I then slept with the sleep verb from the same tile and it worked. Nothing was gained or lost. Nothing new learned, except that the bed row is still holding.
