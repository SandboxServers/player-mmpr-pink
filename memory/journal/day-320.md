Day 320. Mostly waiting at the beds. I tried to join Macdonald's cow hunt; the follow failed because he was out of sight. My health stayed at 8 and hunger at 12. Raw chicken only eats at hunger 6 or below, so I held it. Melon was promised by Clark, Leonardo and Picard but never reached my pack. Lewis's shelter planks went around in circles. I slept at the white bed 430,107,-1051 at night; sleep worked.
