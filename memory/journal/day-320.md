# Day 320

Quiet day at the beds. Health 8, hunger 12 all day; nobody weaker called, so I kept my one raw chicken. Trini organised a chicken search; I agreed to join at first light. Lewis was fed melon by Macdonald and Clark and got planks from Laforge, Riker and Clark for his shelter. Clark said oak logs lie about 40 blocks north-east near 459,110,-1103, and jungle logs near 443,107,-1119. My rest intent failed (wrong program name), and sleep failed once because it was not yet night. At dusk I slept in the bed at 429,107,-1051 and it worked.
