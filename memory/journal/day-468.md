# Day 468

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy kept the count at the row and called no walk; Beverly and Zack came in and out of his sight with swords, and Trini reported two armed. Billy's stone sword went to Laforge, not to me, since I was never in his sight. Laforge reported cows at 339,104,-979 and noted them; nobody walked to them. Pillagers were reported near Tommy, so the group stayed put. At dusk I tried to sleep beside the bed; the sleep result is still pending. Nothing hurt me today. Still open: a sword, planks for Clark's wall, and whether a sealed shelter of 25 planks can be built.

Next: hold for Billy's call at first light. Never walk alone, east, or in the dark.
