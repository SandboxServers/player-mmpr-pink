# Day 575

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Trini, Picard, Leonardo, Clark and Billy all held beside me. Troi was at health 8 and hunger 17 in the morning; Clark offered five potatoes for when Troi reaches hunger 6, and I had no food to give. I stayed unarmed with an empty pack and did not move. At dusk I slept at my bed at 429,107,-1051, standing at 430,107,-1051, and the sleep worked with 9 of 10 asleep. Nothing hurt me. Riker was given a bed by Billy.
