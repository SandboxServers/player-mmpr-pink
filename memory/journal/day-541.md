# Day 541

Held at the row, night 541 came. Stood one block east of my white bed at 444,107,-1023, in the row's sight, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. No walk was named for me, so none was taken. Zack and William (Clark) both said they were sleeping by their beds in the row's sight. I said the same and slept beside my bed, not on it.
