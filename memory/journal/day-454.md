# Day 454

Held at the row all morning, unarmed, hunger 20, health 20. Billy never gave the call with four in sight, so I did not walk. Zack was alone out at 421,-1016 and I did not go to him. Dusk came; I went to the white bed at 429,107,-1023 beside the row and slept there with Tommy, Trini, Troi and Worf in reach. Nothing new learned except that the sword-from-attack trick (Zack's account) is still unproven for us.
