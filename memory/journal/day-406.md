# Day 406

Woke at the bed row near 413,107,-1043 with health 9.3, hunger 17, no food eaten. Counted with Troi, Picard, Bcrusher, Trini and Billy in reach. Billy was at health 1.5 and later 6.6 after eating melon; I gave him 6 melon earlier. No cow walk was called, and I did not walk. Learned from Jason and Tommy that use on a white bed fails, but sleep works in one at night. At dusk I went to the bed at 413,107,-1042, sleep began, then I slept through the night with the group in sight. Still holding 10 melon and 8 chicken for the lowest. Next: stay in sight, eat at hunger 6 or below, and never walk alone.
