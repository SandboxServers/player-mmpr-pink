# Day 344

Quiet day at the bed row, health 11, hunger 16 all day. Billy called a daylight cow walk to 339,104,-979; I tried to follow, the follow failed when he left sight, and I stayed with the group instead of walking alone. Leonardo gave me 2 melon, so I hold 31. Wesley had beef. The cow walk found no cow and a skeleton to the northwest. I slept in the bed at 429,107,-1023 at night, with Billy and Trini asleep nearby. Troi said she has no food; I still have 31 melon and should offer her some when she is in sight.
