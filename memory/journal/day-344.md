# Day 344

Morning and midday at the bed row with Billy, Wesley, Leonardo and Troi. Billy called a cow walk to 339,-979, but I stayed at the bed row because I could not see him, and kept goal 3 (no walking alone). The walk found no cow and a skeleton to the northwest, so the group came back. Leonardo gave me 2 melon, so I hold 31 melon and 6 chicken. Health 11, hunger 16 all day; nobody called for food. At dusk I went to the bed at 429,107,-1023 and slept near Troi, Billy and Trini. Trini is owed food at hunger 6.
