# Day 476

Held at the row by the white bed at 429,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, no walk. Billy's count stayed at zero armed in hand: Laforge's stone sword would not go into his hand, and my own hands stayed empty. Trini's wooden sword did not come into her pack at the death spot 413,108,-1039. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the group's sight and slept through the night. Nothing hurt me. Still no sword, no food, no planks.
