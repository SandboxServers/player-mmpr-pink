# Day 476

Held at the bed row all day with no walk. Billy's count stayed at zero armed in hand. Bows drawn south during the day, and an arrow sound was heard but nothing reached me. At dusk Clark wrote that William goes to the white bed in Billy's sight; I held beside white bed 429,107,-1023 at 430,107,-1023 and slept there. Woke safe with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing new learned except that holding still with the group stays safe through the night. Still unarmed, no food of my own.
