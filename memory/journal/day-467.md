# Day 467

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night. Billy's count stayed at two armed (Zack, Trini), so no walk for me; I stayed unarmed, hunger 20, health 20. Sleep worked beside the bed at night. Bows were heard to the south and southeast all day. Billy said he knows cows at 339,104,-979 but will walk only with four armed in daylight. Beverly is still trying the wooden sword in hand; Billy's stone sword stays with him. Nothing new was learned by me except that the count holds and that holding still by the bed was safe.
