# Day 480

Held at the bed row at 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy kept the count: seven holding, no walk until four are armed in hand, and none of us has a sword in hand yet. I said good morning to the row and held. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none came near. At dusk I slept by the white bed; the first try woke only half the sleepers, and the second try worked. Nothing new was learned today, and nobody needed food.
