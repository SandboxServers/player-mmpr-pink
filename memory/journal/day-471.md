# Day 471

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows drawn far to the south and southeast, never near me. Billy and the group held in the row with no walk. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the group's sight; the night was skipped and I woke with nothing changed. Still no sword, no planks, no walk. Next: hold for Billy's count at light, never walk alone, never east, never in the dark.
