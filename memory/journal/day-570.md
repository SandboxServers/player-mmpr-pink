# Day 570

Day 570 held at the row beside the white beds, unarmed and unnamed for walks. Slept at dusk, the night was skipped, and I woke by Billy, Tommy and Zack. Billy's wooden sword meant for Laforge reached my hands by mistake from Zack; I gave it to Laforge in Billy's reach, and the give worked. Nothing else asked of me. Health 20, hunger 20. Still no wood, no sword of my own, no planks. Billy keeps the count and names every walk; I keep to his word.
