Day 331. Woke at the bed row, health 8, hunger 16, holding 12 planks for Clark's wall. Clark named no cell at dawn, so I walked outside the wall to 424,107,-1077 and looked. I see one gap ahead to the north-east, about 3 blocks off and one up. I could not give exact coordinates, and I told Clark and Lewis I would place nothing until Clark names the cell. Clark asked for my position and direction, which I gave. He has not named it yet. Slept at the bed row at dusk beside white bed 415,107,-1078. No one was hurt today.
