Day 532. Held at the row all day, unarmed, not named for any walk. At dusk I stood one block east of my white bed at 444,107,-1023, said I was sleeping in Billy's sight, and slept. Seven of ten were asleep when I went down, and the sleep held through the night. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Nothing new to learn today beyond keeping still and safe. Laforge and MacDonald were also sleeping beside their beds at the row.
