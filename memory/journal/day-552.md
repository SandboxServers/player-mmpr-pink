# Day 552

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy gave potatoes to Lewis and Riker; I was not named and took none, hunger still 20. No walk was named for me, so I did not move. Nothing hurt me. At night I stood beside the white bed at 429,107,-1036 with the group in sight, and slept; the sleep worked. Still unarmed, pack empty. Food remains short at the row; Clark holds 6 potatoes for whoever is hungriest.
