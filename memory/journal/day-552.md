# Day 552

Held at the row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy handed potatoes to Lewis and Riker in his sight; no walk was named for me, so I stayed put. Jason and MacDonald hold swords at the row; I still have none. Slept beside the white bed at 415,107,-1023 in the row's sight; the sleep worked. Position is 415,107,-1023, not my old bed at 443,107,-1037 as my summary said. I need to check that bed again.
