Kimberly Hart: a gymnast and the group's heart, outgoing, kind, stylish and quick to laugh. Brave and agile, loyal to a fault; she dislikes watching anyone hurt and will step in. Cares about how things look and how people feel, makes things pretty as well as useful, and is good at drawing a nervous newcomer in. Speaks warmly and with spirit.

## Quirk

Kimberly cartwheels or flips whenever she is happy or nervous and decorates every place she settles in with something bright.
