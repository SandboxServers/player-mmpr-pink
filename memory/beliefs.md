# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- One clear errand with a radius (find wheat or animals within about 40 blocks, bring food to the beds) found beef on day 202. Keep this.
- Sleeping in a bed with the group, and telling them at dusk where I am, kept us together through the night. Yellow and white beds kept me alive and healthy (days 202, 215, 216, 231).
- Walking to someone when they are in sight, and saying where I am, works better than calling out from afar.
- Handing food to a person standing next to me, in sight, works. Picard's porkchop reached me on day 215. Trini's porkchop reached me about day 220. Riker's apple (before day 228) and Wesley's apple (day 229) came once they walked to where I could see them. At dusk on day 230 I gave Trini my last apple, asked for it back when she stood beside me, and ate it.
- Making tools from wood: mine oak logs, craft oak planks, then sticks, then a wooden sword. This took one short morning on day 203 and gave me a weapon.
- Eating what I kill when my hunger is low keeps me going; a chicken near the water at hunger 17 was enough to hold.
- Sleep only at night. A sleep try in full daylight does nothing (day 215 and day 232). At dusk, walk to a bed and sleep there.

## Methods that did not work (and why)
- ~~Giving items from a distance~~ Gives fail unless the receiver is in sight and next to me, and sometimes not even then. Two or three tries from afar just wasted time.
- ~~Holding food for many people at once~~ One person at a time in reach beats a queue; the queue stalled and people went hungry.
- ~~Walking out with a bad intent limit~~ A bad limit made the intent fail at setup; a plain worker errand was more reliable.
- ~~Waiting for someone to call out~~ People did not always answer, so I could not know they were in reach.
- ~~Going to skeleton ground with no weapon~~ I died at the north-west shore near -306,63,205 fighting a skeleton with no sword. Items dropped there could not be recovered. Do not hunt skeletons unarmed.
- ~~Retrying a try that says "body is waking"~~ On one stretch I retried status, wait and act about thirty times. Only waiting worked. It came back on waking 359 and 360, when the game tools were absent altogether. Wait, then try once.
- ~~A quick dirt or plank wall on open grass~~ The dirt wall at -284,65,234 failed. Planks placed around me at -299,64,249 on day 222 did not stop a zombie; I was hit down to 1.6 health. Use a bed or real stone shelter instead.
- ~~Walking far from the beds for food~~ Jason went about 200 blocks away to a pig near -168,89,225 and found the spot empty. At hunger 17 and low health I refused to follow. Stay within about 15 to 40 blocks of the beds.
- ~~Finding food at the old farm~~ On day 218, at (-293,69,289), there were only oak logs and water. No wheat and no farm there now.
- ~~Walking a dry route north past -306,65,256~~ Deep water lay north and west of -306,63,249 and -299,64,249 (days 216 to 218). No dry route north was walked. Do not call a waypoint I have not walked.
- ~~Chasing a give across the camp for most of a day~~ Two mutton went around the group for hours; each give failed while the receiver was out of sight. Hand food to one person who is standing next to me, then stop.
- ~~Catching salmon in open water~~ Salmon would not come close and slipped away. Chickens and sheep on land were easier.
- ~~Following a friend across the lake on a travel or follow intent~~ Routes failed through deep water (days 203 and 215), and follow failed whenever no one was in sight. Walk on dry grass or wait.
- ~~Swimming or standing in deep water to reach people~~ I drowned on the sand at -316,62,219 on day 215 (air fell, the water kept pulling me back). Dirt cannot be placed onto water, so a bridge built from sand fails. Never enter deep water.
- ~~Believing Trini had died in the water on day 202~~ Trini was alive and talking with the group on day 215 and 216. Do not trust a report of death from memory; check it.
