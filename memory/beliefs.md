# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Sending a worker on one clear errand with a radius (find wheat or animals within about 40 blocks, bring food to the beds) found beef. Keep this.
- Sleeping in a bed with the group, and telling them at dusk where I am, kept us together through the night.
- Walking to someone when they are in sight, and saying where I am, works better than calling out from afar.
- Sleeping in a real bed (yellow or white beds, day 202) kept me alive and healthy through the night. A bed is the safe place.
- Eating what I kill when my hunger is low keeps me going; a chicken near the water at hunger 17 was enough to hold.

## Methods that did not work (and why)
- ~~Giving items from a distance~~ Gives fail unless the receiver is in sight and next to me, and sometimes not even then. Two or three tries from afar just wasted time.
- ~~Holding food for many people at once~~ One person at a time in reach beats a queue; the queue stalled and people went hungry.
- ~~Walking out with a bad intent limit~~ A bad limit made the intent fail at setup; a plain worker errand was more reliable.
- ~~Waiting for someone to call out~~ People did not always answer, so I could not know they were in reach.
- ~~Going to skeleton ground with no weapon~~ I died at the north-west shore near -306,63,205 fighting a skeleton with no sword. Items dropped there could not be recovered. Do not hunt skeletons unarmed.
- ~~Retrying a try that says "body is waking"~~ On days 316 to 322 I retried status, wait and act about thirty times. Only waiting worked. Wait, then try once.
- ~~A quick dirt wall on open grass at night~~ The wall at -284,65,234 failed. Use a bed or real stone shelter instead.
- ~~Chasing a give across the camp for most of a day~~ Two mutton went around the group for hours; each give failed while the receiver was out of sight. Hand food to one person who is standing next to me, then stop.
- ~~Catching salmon in open water~~ Salmon would not come close and slipped away. Chickens and sheep on land were easier.
