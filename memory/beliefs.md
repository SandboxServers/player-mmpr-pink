# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Sending a worker on one clear errand with a radius (find wheat or animals within about 40 blocks, bring food to the beds) found beef. Keep this.
- Sleeping in a bed with the group, and telling them at dusk where I am, kept us together through the night.
- Walking to someone when they are in sight, and saying where I am, works better than calling out from afar.

## Methods that did not work (and why)
- ~~Giving items from a distance~~ Gives fail unless the receiver is in sight and next to me, and sometimes not even then. Two or three tries from afar just wasted time.
- ~~Holding food for many people at once~~ One person at a time in reach beats a queue; the queue stalled and people went hungry.
- ~~Walking out with a bad intent limit~~ A bad limit made the intent fail at setup; a plain worker errand was more reliable.
- ~~Waiting for someone to call out~~ People did not always answer, so I could not know they were in reach.

## What I hold to be true
- Food is the problem that matters most here. Cows near (-313,81,369) and wheat near the farm around (-293,69,288) have been the only sources found so far. Golems guard the farm.
- Iron golems and villagers can attack, and so can zombies at night. Being in stone or a bed before dark keeps me alive.
- Health and hunger are the two numbers to watch. Eat when hunger drops, and do not wait until it is critical.
