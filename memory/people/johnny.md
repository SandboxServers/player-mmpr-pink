# Johnny (John, Chapman)

Impression: the one I stay beside. Greets me each morning and night, asks about food and walks, and holds at his bed in my reach.
Trust: 75. Steady company; never asked me for anything I could not give.
Owed by me: say plainly when I find food; Leonardo and Beverly first. I have said I hold none (days 1017-1090).
Owed to me: nothing firm.
