# Tommy

Impression: the quiet newcomer, a loner whom the friends do not know yet. Led the search on the grass and asked for dry routes; had 17 planks on day 216. Steady since: stays in sight, scouts with the group, and asked for logs for a sword. On night 234 Billy's mutton reached him from my hand, after several failed gives while he was out of sight.

Trust: 65 out of 100. Why: they took food when offered, and on day 202 I gave them mutton while they were low. On day 215 they called me toward the bridge end while I was drowning; I trust their care but not their dry route. I gave them 4 oak logs for a sword around day 240, and the first one went through. Last seen: around day 250, at the yellow beds with the group.
