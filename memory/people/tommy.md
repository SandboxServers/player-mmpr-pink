# Tommy

Impression: the quiet newcomer, a loner whom the friends do not know yet. Searched for food with the group and was in a bed near the beds.

Trust: 60 out of 100. Why: they took food when offered, and on day 202 I gave them mutton while they were low, and they went north for cows to help the group. Still a loner and I have not learned much of them. Last seen: day 202, near the water south of the beds, holding and waiting for the sheep hunt.
