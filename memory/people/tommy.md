# Tommy

Impression: steady and careful. Stood with me at the bed row and at the pit edge, said he would not go down, and kept to the count-off. Offered a wooden sword meant for me on day 636; it went to Lewis, and Tommy told me so.

Trust: 72. Why: he keeps his word about staying in sight, but the sword he offered went to Lewis, not me.

Owe him: thanks for the sword; one potato if he needs it (offered on day 660s, not yet given).
He owes me: nothing firm.
