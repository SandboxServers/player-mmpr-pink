# Tommy

Impression: the quiet newcomer, a loner whom the friends do not know yet. Led the search on the grass and asked for dry routes; had 17 planks on day 216. Since then, steady: stays in sight, scouts the grass with the group, and calls out what it sees.

Trust: 60 out of 100. Why: they took food when offered, and on day 202 I gave them mutton while they were low. On day 215 they were out on the lake side while I was drowning, and they called me toward the bridge end; I trust their care but not their dry route yet. They have not given me food, and I have not asked. Last seen: day 228, scouting south-west with me and Jason.
