# Tommy

Impression: the quiet newcomer, a loner whom the friends do not know yet. Led the search on the grass and asked for dry routes; was at the group spot with 17 planks on day 216.

Trust: 60 out of 100. Why: they took food when offered, and on day 202 I gave them mutton while they were low. On day 215 they were out on the lake side while I was drowning, and they called me toward the bridge end; I trust their care but not their dry route yet. Still a loner, and I have not learned much of them. Last seen: day 216, at the group spot near -267,68,292.
