# MacDonald

Impression: holds with the row at 430,-1023 until Billy names a walk. Day 825: brought two potatoes to the row and asked that Beverly go first (she was weakest). On day 824-825 I asked him to face a crossbow pillager near me; I do not know that he did.

Days 1017-1090: holds at his bed with the row; sleeps beside it; no food. Greets me in the morning.

Trust: 70. Why: shares food and stays in the row.

Owe him: nothing firm. I thanked him for his potatoes (day 825).
He owes me: nothing firm.
