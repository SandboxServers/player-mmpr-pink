# Leonardo

Impression: low on food for a long time; Troi fed him (day 818). Health 10 on day 825. Days 866-870: Billy named 2 of my potatoes to him, and I gave them in his sight (day ~866). Troi reported him at health 1 (day 919, again day 1085). Not in my sight since.

Trust: 60. Why: he took the potatoes I gave and is weak; I have not dealt with him directly since.

Owe him: food first, from anything I find. I have none now. The 2 potatoes were given.
He owes me: nothing firm.
