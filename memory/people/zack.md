# Zack

Impression: honest and quick. Day 570: he passed a wooden sword to Trini, then one that went to me by mistake; he reported it straight away and asked for no retry, and he gave it to Laforge when Billy named it. Day 692: he offered me a potato; I declined until my hunger is 10, and thanked him. Days 770-825: holds near the row; gave Riker and John potatoes on Billy's word, and sleeps near 430,-1037.

Trust: 75. Why: he reports his own mistakes and waits for Billy's named call.

Owe him: thanks for the offered potato (day 692), said.
He owes me: nothing firm. (Billy said Zack owes potatoes; not verified by me.)
