# Billy

Impression: works out how things work. Scouted the north with me on day 216 and the grass on day 223. Often the hungriest of the group. On day 234 he gave Jason 2 mutton and held 1 for Tommy, sharing before himself. He was hit by phantoms and respawned with nothing on day 233.

Trust: 65 out of 100. Why: they went looking for a dry route with me, kept food for others rather than eat it, and offered me a bed spot on day 215. Gives to them failed more than once before. Last seen: day 242, dead in the sheep field north of the beds. Respawn status unknown since then.
