# Billy

Impression: keeps the count and names walks and beds aloud. Clear and careful. Gave food to those low (Troi, day 581; me, a potato at the chest, day 654). Slow to name walks. His named beds refused me on days 698, 749, 764, 765 and 766. Died to pillagers north-west near 411,-1056 on day 764; speaks again from day 765. Days 866-965: named the potatoes (2 to Leonardo, 4 to Trini) and walks toward his bed at dusk that met phantoms. Days 1017-1090: named nothing to me; silent to me on food, walks and beds. Last seen near 430,107,-1037 (day ~866).

Trust: 60 (down from 65). Why: his word on holding has held and he has fed people, but his named beds refused me, his dusk walk (day ~866) cost me my sword and my potatoes, and he has named nothing for me for about 70 days.

Owe him: report my position on every wake. Told him on days 1041 and 1076 that my potatoes went out (2 to Leonardo, 4 to Trini) and the sword is lost at 421,107,-1047: done. No walk without his named call.
He owes me: a named bed that takes me (429,107,-1050 named, untried; 429,107,-1023 named, untried); a verified west route if he has one; a named walk or food for me when he is able.
