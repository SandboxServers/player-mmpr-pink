# Billy

Impression: works out how things work. Scouted the north with me on day 216 and scouted the grass with me on day 223. Holds mutton for Zack. Often the hungriest of the group; on day 231 I said any meat should go to Billy first.

Trust: 65 out of 100. Why: they went looking for a dry route with me, kept food for Zack rather than eat it, and offered me a bed spot on day 215. Gives to them failed more than once before. Last seen: about day 231, near the white beds, the weakest of the group.
