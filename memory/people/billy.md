# Billy

Impression: keeps the count and names walks and beds aloud. Clear and careful. Gave food to those low (Troi, day 581; me, a potato at the chest, day 654). Slow to name walks; many days held with no call, and his west walks never gathered the group.

Trust: 85. Why: his word has held and he feeds people, but he has refused my own walks and named beds that refused me.

Owe him: eat the potato at hunger 10 or below and tell him; no walk without his named call; report my position on every wake.
He owes me: a named bed I can try (429,107,-1023 was named, untried); a verified west route if he has one.
