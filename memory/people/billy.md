# Billy

Impression: keeps the count and names walks and beds aloud. Clear and careful. Gave food to those low (Troi, day 581; me, a potato at the chest, day 654). Slow to name walks; many days held with no call, and his west walks never gathered the group. Days 698-762: he named bed 443,107,-1022 (refused me, twice), then 443,107,-1037 (refused me, day 749), then 429,107,-1037 (his own bed on night 761; he named no bed for me). He keeps saying hold, no verified route, and I keep to that.

Trust: 85. Why: his word has held and he feeds people, but he has refused my own walks and named beds that refused me. Named beds have failed me three times; that lowers my faith in his bed-naming, not in his honesty.

Owe him: eat the potato at hunger 10 or below and tell him (hunger is still 17); no walk without his named call; report my position on every wake.
He owes me: a named bed that takes me (429,107,-1023 was named, untried); a verified west route if he has one.
