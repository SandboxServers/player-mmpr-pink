# Billy

Impression: keeps the count and names walks and beds aloud. Clear and careful. Gave food to those low (Troi, day 581; me, a potato at the chest, day 654). Slow to name walks; many days held with no call. His named beds refused me on days 698, 749, 764, 765 and 766. Died to pillagers north-west near 411,-1056 on day 764; speaks again from day 765. Days 770-825: holds the group at the row, names no walk to me, asks for hunger and any route or sheep news.

Trust: 70. Why: his word on holding has held and he feeds people, but his named beds have refused me each time and his walks have ended in pillager shots.

Owe him: eat the potato at hunger 10 and tell him; no walk without his named call; report my position on every wake.
He owes me: a named bed that takes me (429,107,-1050 named, untried); a verified west route if he has one.
