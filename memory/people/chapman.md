# Chapman (also called John and Johnny)

Impression: in reach at the row for most of days 918-965, holding in the open at 444,107,-1021 as I do. Friendly and steady; we greet each other at every dusk and morning. Holds for Billy's named call as I do; I have not heard him say what he holds.

Trust: 66. Why: steady, present, and says plainly what he holds (nothing); we both wait on Billy's named call.

Owe him: nothing firm.
He owes me: nothing firm.
