# Beverly

Impression: holds in the row with the group; named in the day 694 talk with Billy about routes. Days 770-825: weakest in the row, health 5 (day 824), hunger 17 (day 825) with potatoes offered by MacDonald. Asked me for food; I had none and said so. Days 866-965: named by Billy as first to receive my 4 potatoes, but she was never in my sight, so the give failed and the 4 went to Trini.

Trust: 50. Why: I have not been able to reach her in sight; she asked for food and I could not give it.

Owe her: nothing firm. I owed her 4 potatoes (Billy's order, day ~866); they went to Trini instead when Beverly could not be found. I am sorry I could not give them (day 965).
She owes me: nothing firm.
