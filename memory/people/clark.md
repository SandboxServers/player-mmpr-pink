# Clark

Leads the plank wall on the west side. Careful and slow to name cells. Day 331: he could not read the open cells from inside, so he asked me, outside, to give my position and the direction of the gap. Day 332: I gave him the gap cell 423,107,-1078; he named it for Lewis, and he found no open gap by dusk. Trust: 77. He is honest about what he cannot see and does not guess.
