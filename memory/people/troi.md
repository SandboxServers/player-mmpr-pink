# Troi

Also called Deanna in the journal (same person by context).

Impression: in the row with the group; fed by Billy on day 581 when low. Fed Leonardo (day 818). Days 918-1090: at her bed; reports Leonardo at health 1 (day 919, again day 1085) and Zack at 13 (day 1035). Greets me often and asks how I hold. Steady and careful: walks nowhere until a daylight cell is named and seen.

Trust: 70. Why: present, friendly, honest news; we hold together.

Owe her: "if you need my potato sooner, say so." I hold no potato, so I owe her only my word to tell her first if I find food.
She owes me: nothing firm.
