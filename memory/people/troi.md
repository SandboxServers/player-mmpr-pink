# Troi

Impression: in the row with the group; fed by Billy on day 581 when low. Days 695-762: at 14 health and hunger 16 once (day 695); later steady beside the beds with Trini and me. Greets me each morning and asks how I am.

Trust: 60. Why: present and friendly; I have had little direct dealing with her.

Owe her: "if you need my potato sooner, say so" (offered to Billy about Troi at hunger 16, day 716s). I still hold the potato for my own hunger 10.
She owes me: nothing firm.
