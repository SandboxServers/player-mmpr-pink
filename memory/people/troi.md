# Troi

Also called Deanna in the journal (same person by context).

Impression: in the row with the group; fed by Billy on day 581 when low. Days 695-762: at health 14 and hunger 16 once (day 695). Days 810-825: greets me each morning, asks how I am, and holds beside me by the beds. Fed Leonardo (day 818). Days 918-965: at her bed; reports Leonardo at health 1 (day 919); greets me often and I greet her back.

Trust: 68. Why: present, friendly, steady; asks after me and after Leonardo; we hold together.

Owe her: "if you need my potato sooner, say so." I now hold no potato (all given out by day 965), so I owe her only my word to tell her if I find food.
She owes me: nothing firm.
