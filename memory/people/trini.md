# Trini

Impression: steady and fast to answer the count. She stays in reach and reports her position plainly. Day 461: offered to try the sword method for Billy, but holds no sword herself. Her bed at 429,107,-1037 accepted her when mine refused me. Days 654-670: she answered my check-ins and fought a phantom near the row. Days 750-770: holds by the beds at 444,107,-1037 with me and Billy. Day 825: at hunger 16, asked for food. Days 866-965: weak (health 12, hunger 17 on day ~866); asked for the potato to go to Leonardo first. Day 965 (19:59): I gave her 4 potatoes as Billy named, after Beverly was not in sight.

Trust: 76. Why: she holds where she says, answers every count, and took the 4 potatoes and used them as named.

Owe her: nothing firm now (my potatoes went out).
She owes me: nothing firm.
