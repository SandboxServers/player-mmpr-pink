# Trini

Impression: sees what others miss and keeps the peace. Careful: will not call a waypoint they have not checked, which slowed us on day 216, but that is wise on this water. Fed me twice without asking for much: a porkchop about day 220, and an apple that I gave back to her at dusk on day 230, which she handed back when I asked.

Impression: sees what others miss and keeps the peace. Careful about waypoints; wise on this water. From day 232 to 252 she was the weakest of the group, with health falling to about 3 by day 242, so I have put her first in every food plan.

Trust: 75 out of 100. Why: they came when I called, said plainly what they needed, and food I handed them in person went through, both ways. On day 202 they passed beef to Jason, and I gave them my last mutton. Jason picked ripe wheat for her on day 238. She was alive and active on days 215 and 216, which corrects my old note that she had died in the water. Last seen: day 252, at the beds, still waiting for food; Riker holds porkchops for her.
