# Trini

Impression: steady and fast to answer the count. She stays in reach and reports her position plainly. Day 461: offered to try the sword method for Billy, but holds no sword herself. Her bed at 429,107,-1037 accepted her when mine refused me. Days 654-670: she answered my check-ins and fought a phantom near the row. Days 750-770: holds by the beds at 444,107,-1037 with me and Billy, and answers when I call. Day 770: spoke to me, and I answered. Day 825: at hunger 16, asked for food; I said I hold one potato for the weakest in reach.

Trust: 74. Why: she holds where she says and answers every count; earlier she kept a melon slice back, so I still ask plainly.

Owe her: my potato, if she is the weakest in reach when Billy names a path to her (said day 825).
She owes me: nothing firm.
