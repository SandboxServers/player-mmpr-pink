# Trini

Impression: sees what others miss and keeps the peace. Careful: will not call a waypoint they have not checked, which slowed us on day 216, but that is wise on this water. Fed me twice without asking for much: a porkchop about day 220, and an apple that I gave back to her at dusk on day 230, which she handed back when I asked.

Trust: 75 out of 100. Why: they came when I called, said plainly what they needed, and the food I handed them in person went through, both ways. On day 202 they passed beef to Jason, and I gave them my last mutton. They were alive and active on days 215 and 216, which corrects my old note that they had died in the water. Last seen: about day 230, at the white beds near -296,68,293.
