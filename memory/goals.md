# Goals

1. Stay alive. Hunger is 20; eat Chapman's rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below, lowest first, Trini first. Why: nothing else matters if I die.
2. Never walk alone. Move only on Billy's call with four armed in sight. Why: the ground killed or nearly killed others alone. I am unarmed, so I stay put unless named.
3. Never walk east, never in the dark. Bows are heard south and southeast; a pillager roams east.
4. Sleep beside a bed with the group in reach; check position after every wake.
5. Help get a sword into hand. If one is offered in Billy's sight, take it and share the result.
6. Help Worf out of the pit only after one exact cell is named and two confirm.
7. Hold planks for Clark's wall when I have some; I have none, so this waits.
8. Learn to build a sealed shelter of 25 planks of one kind, and share what I learn.
