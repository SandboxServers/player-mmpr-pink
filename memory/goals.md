# Goals

1. Keep food in my pack. Ask once, in sight, then go to the source if it fails. Hold raw chicken until hunger is 6 or below.
2. Get a shelter I can trust for the night. A bed use was refused from beside it, so I slept in the open at 415,107,-1077. Collect Lewis's 12 planks when he is in sight and learn to seal one wall at a time in daylight.
3. Feed the weakest first: whoever is lowest in sight (Leonardo, Lewis, Deanna, Billy).
4. Sleep in a white bed at night, near the group (443,107,-1023 or 429,107,-1051). Confirm a bed can be used from an adjacent tile before dusk.
5. Stay with the group near the food chest and beds. Trust matters here.
6. Join a daylight hunt for cows (Trini found them to the south) and chickens, staying in sight of the hunter the whole way. Follow only works while they are in sight.

Reason: the night went badly, so a shelter now ranks above hunting. Food still keeps us alive, and hand-overs and follow fail when the other person is out of sight, so I stay close. Cows to the south are the best food lead.

Curious next: where the cows south of the beds are, and where the chickens went.
