# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat at hunger 6 or below. Reason: hunger is 17 and health 6.3; health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
2. Feed Trini first at hunger 6, then whoever is lowest. Reason: the group depends on the food I hold (about 31 melon, 6 chicken).
3. Never walk alone; stay in sight of the group, especially at the cow field and after dusk. Reason: I was separated before and nearly died.
4. Help Worf out of the gravel pit once he names one exact stone cell and two of us see it in good light. Do not dig blind, and never go down alone. Reason: he is alone in the pit and has no food.
5. Sleep only with the group in reach beside a bed; check position after every wake. Reason: sleep has left me at other beds before.
6. Cow walk only when Billy calls it and everyone is in sight.
7. Hold 12 oak planks for Clark's wall; place only on a cell he names.
8. Learn to build a sealed shelter of 25 planks of one kind, and share what I learn.
