# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat at hunger 6 or below. Reason: health 11, hunger 17, and health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
2. Keep food for Trini first at 6, then whoever is lowest. Reason: the group depends on this food.
3. Stay in sight of the group; no walking alone. Reason: a separated walker cannot be helped.
4. Sleep only with the group in reach, and check position after waking. Reason: sleep has pulled me to other beds before. Last night the bed at 401,107,-1022 kept me near Tommy.
5. Help Worf out of the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022 and bring him to the beds with food if he drops. Reason: he has no food and is alone.
6. Cow walk at first light with Billy, only when he calls it and everyone is in sight.
7. Hold 12 oak planks for Clark's wall; place only on a cell he names.
8. Learn to build a sealed shelter of my own (25 planks of one kind), and share what I learn.
