# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat only at hunger 6 or below unless health is critical. Reason: health is 8 and does not regenerate while hunger is low, so eating at the wrong time wastes food and gains nothing.
2. Keep 20 melon and 1 chicken for the weakest. Trini first at 6, then Jason. Reason: the group depends on this food when someone drops low, and Jason is still owed melon.
3. Stay in reach of the group; no walking alone. Reason: bows are heard to the south, and a lone walker cannot be helped or give help.
4. Hold 12 oak planks for Clark's wall; place only on a cell he names. Reason: Clark asked for the wall and only exact cells are safe to use.
5. Join daylight cow hunts only when the group is in sight. Reason: the last cow walk broke up and Trini died at the cow ground.
6. Learn to build a sealed shelter of my own, and share what I learn. Reason: a shelter of my own gives safety at night apart from the bed row.
