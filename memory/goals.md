# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep health up, stay near the bed row, and avoid skeleton lines of fire.
2. Hold 12 oak planks for Clark's wall. Place only on a cell he names.
3. Keep 18 melon and 1 raw chicken for the weakest in sight. Eat only at hunger 6 or below.
4. Sleep in a white bed at night, from an adjacent tile if needed.
5. Join a daylight cow or sheep hunt when health is safe, staying in sight of the group.
6. Learn to build a sealed shelter of 25 blocks of one kind, after the wall is done.

Reason: survival and trust come first; the wall needs Clark's word; food matters for others more than for me.

Curious next: where the cows and sheep graze now, and how Clark's wall is meant to close.
