# Goals

1. Keep food in my pack. Ask once, in sight, then go to the source if it fails. Hold raw chicken until hunger is 6 or below.
2. Feed the weakest first: whoever is lowest in sight (Lewis, Deanna, Billy).
3. Sleep in a white bed at night, near the group (443,107,-1023 or 429,107,-1051).
4. Stay with the group near the food chest and beds. Trust matters here.
5. Join a daylight cow or chicken hunt; find where chickens and cows are.
6. Find out how to seal a shelter that holds. Test one wall at a time.

Reason for the change: hand-overs fail when the giver is out of sight, so I stay in view and accept melon in reach.
