# Goals

1. Stay alive. I have no food now: my potatoes went to Leonardo, Trini and Bcrusher. Eat only when someone gives me food, or if I find some. Why: hunger is 20 now, but without food I will starve.
2. Never walk alone. Move only on Billy's named call with the group in sight. Why: I died walking alone on day 622, and after a reflex walk on day 766.
3. Keep out of pillager sight. Hold at 444,107,-1021 or near the row until Billy names a walk. Why: pillagers and bows keep coming from the southwest and west (heard again on day 915).
4. Help the weakest in reach with food when I get some. Leonardo is at health 1 (per Troi, day 915); I have nothing to give now. Why: I promised to hand food to whoever is weakest.
5. Sleep sheltered. Try a bed only when Billy names it at night. Refused: 429,107,-1036, 429,107,-1037, 443,107,-1022, 443,107,-1036, 443,107,-1037, 415,107,-1036, 415,107,-1037. Named but untried: 429,107,-1050. Why: I must rest, and days since sleep is 329.
6. Keep the wooden sword in my pack. Put it in my hand only when Billy names it. Why: the bows keep coming, and I have been unarmed.
7. Never walk east in the dark, never north-west. Why: pillagers and crossbows west and north-west, and one shot me south of the row on day 825.
8. Help Worf out of the pit only after one exact cell is named and two confirm. Worf unseen since day 585.
9. Hold planks for Clark's wall when I have some (none now).
10. Learn a verified route west with the group before anyone walks it; share what I learn.
11. Later: build a sealed shelter of 25 planks of one kind.

Owed (keep until done):
- Thank Lewis and Tommy for the wooden sword (not yet said in the journal).
- Tell Billy that my potatoes went out (to Leonardo, Trini, and Bcrusher's four routed through me), since he named them.
- Offer food to Troi or Leonardo first if I ever find some.
