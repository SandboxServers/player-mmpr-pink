# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat only at hunger 6 or below unless health is critical. Health 8.
2. Keep 20 melon and 1 chicken for the weakest in the group. Trini first at 6, then Jason.
3. Stay in reach of the group; no walking alone. Do not leave the bed row without everyone in sight.
4. Hold 12 oak planks for Clark's wall; place only on a cell he names.
5. Join daylight cow hunts only when the group is in sight.
6. Learn to build a sealed shelter of my own, and share what I learn.
