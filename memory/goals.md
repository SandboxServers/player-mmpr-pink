# Goals

1. Stay alive. I hold no food and no sword: my wooden sword was taken by phantoms at 421,107,-1047 (day ~866), and my last potatoes went to Leonardo (2) and Trini (4). Eat only when someone gives me food, or if I find some. Why: hunger is 20 now, but without food I will starve.
2. Never walk alone. Move only on Billy's named call with the group in sight. Never walk at dusk: the dusk walk toward Billy's bed on day ~866 cost me my sword, my potatoes and nearly my life. Why: I died walking alone on day 622, and after a reflex walk on day 766.
3. Keep out of pillager and phantom sight. Hold at 444,107,-1021 in reach of Chapman and Troi until Billy names a walk or a bed. Why: bows keep coming from the southwest and west (heard through day 965).
4. Help the weakest in reach with food when I get some. Leonardo was reported at health 1 (Troi, day 919); I have nothing to give. Why: I promised to hand food to whoever is weakest.
5. Sleep sheltered. Try a bed only when Billy names it at night. Refused: 429,107,-1036, 429,107,-1037, 443,107,-1022, 443,107,-1036, 443,107,-1037, 415,107,-1036, 415,107,-1037. Named but untried: 429,107,-1050, 429,107,-1023. Why: I must rest, and I have not slept in a bed for a long time.
6. Get a sword back into my hand when Billy names one (none in my pack now). Why: the bows and phantoms keep coming, and I have been unarmed since day ~866.
7. Never walk east in the dark, never north-west. Why: pillagers and crossbows west and north-west, and phantoms at dusk.
8. Help Worf out of the pit only after one exact cell is named and two confirm. Worf unseen since day 585.
9. Hold planks for Clark's wall when I have some (none now).
10. Learn a verified route west with the group before anyone walks it; share what I learn. Route 419,108,-1047 blocked day ~866.
11. Later: build a sealed shelter of 25 planks of one kind (none now).

Owed (keep until done):
- Tell Billy plainly: my potatoes went out (2 to Leonardo, 4 to Trini, after Beverly could not be found), and my sword is lost at 421,107,-1047.
- Thank Lewis and Tommy for the wooden sword (not yet said in the journal). The sword is now lost, so the thanks is owed still.
- Thank Bcrusher for the 4 potatoes he gave me (day ~866).
- Beverly: Billy named her first for 4 potatoes; I could not get them to her in sight and gave them to Trini. Owe her food if I find any; say so to her.
- Offer food to Troi or Leonardo first if I ever find some; tell Troi I now hold none.
